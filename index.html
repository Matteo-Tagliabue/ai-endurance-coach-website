Features

One coach across your whole endurance season.

Personalized plans

Weekly training plans adapted to recent load, availability, preferences and upcoming races.

Workout analysis

Completed activities are evaluated using pace, heart rate, power, elevation and athlete feedback.

Multi-sport coaching

Support for running, cycling, swimming and triathlon within one coherent training plan.

Race-focused progression

Training is structured around race dates, target performances, priority levels and long-term continuity.

Strava integration

Recent training data can be synchronized and used to update the athlete profile and coaching context.

Garmin integration

Planned support for publishing structured workouts directly to Garmin Connect after explicit user authorization.

How it works

From training data to the next useful decision.

1

Connect your data

The athlete connects supported training services and defines goals, preferences and race dates.

2

Receive a plan

The coach creates a realistic weekly schedule across endurance sports, including recovery.

3

Train and give feedback

Completed sessions and subjective feedback are combined to understand the athlete's real response.

4

Adapt continuously

Future plans remain coherent with training history instead of reacting blindly to a single workout.
