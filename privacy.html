Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

1. About AI Endurance Coach

AI Endurance Coach is an endurance coaching platform developed by Matteo Tagliabue in Switzerland. It provides personalized training support for running, cycling, swimming and triathlon.

2. Data we may process

Depending on the services connected by the user, AI Endurance Coach may process:

  • basic account and contact information;
  • training activities and physiological metrics;
  • heart-rate, pace, speed, power, distance and elevation data;
  • race dates, goals and training preferences;
  • athlete feedback and coaching conversations;
  • training plans and workout history;
  • authentication tokens required for connected services.

3. Purpose of processing

Data are processed only when necessary to:

  • provide personalized endurance coaching;
  • create and adapt training plans;
  • analyse completed workouts;
  • support connected services requested by the user;
  • maintain account security and technical reliability.

4. Connected services

Users may choose to connect third-party services such as Strava, Intervals.icu or Garmin Connect. Data received from these services are used only for the coaching features explicitly requested by the user and are handled according to the applicable platform terms.

When an athlete connects Intervals.icu, the coach may create and update planned structured workouts in that athlete’s calendar, within the permissions explicitly granted by the athlete.

5. Legal basis

Where applicable, data are processed on the basis of the user's consent, the performance of a requested service, and legitimate interests in maintaining the security and reliability of the platform.

6. Data sharing

Personal data are not sold. Data may be processed by technical service providers only when necessary to operate the platform, such as hosting, messaging, artificial intelligence or connected fitness services.

7. Data retention

Data are retained only for as long as required to provide the service, maintain legitimate records or comply with legal obligations. Users may request deletion of their stored data.

8. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect personal data, including controlled access, secure authentication and separation of user data.

9. User rights

Users may request access, correction, export or deletion of their personal data and may withdraw consent for connected services.
