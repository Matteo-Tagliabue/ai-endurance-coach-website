Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

1. About AI Endurance Coach

AI Endurance Coach is an endurance coaching platform developed by Matteo Tagliabue in Switzerland. It provides personalized training support for running, cycling, swimming and triathlon.

2. Data we may process

Depending on the services connected by the user, AI Endurance Coach may process:

  • basic account and contact information, and profile details provided by the user such as date of birth, weight and height;
  • notification preferences and browser push subscriptions;
  • training activities and physiological metrics;
  • heart-rate, pace, speed, power, distance and elevation data;
  • race dates, goals and training preferences;
  • athlete feedback and coaching conversations;
  • training plans and workout history;
  • authentication tokens required for connected services;
  • device information, activity location and sensor samples where available, and weather estimates associated with an activity.

3. Purpose of processing

Data are processed only when necessary to:

  • provide personalized endurance coaching;
  • create and adapt training plans;
  • analyse completed workouts;
  • support connected services requested by the user;
  • maintain account security and technical reliability.

4. Connected services

Users may choose to connect third-party services such as Strava, Intervals.icu, Garmin Connect (where enabled) or Telegram. Compatible watch platforms may be connected through Intervals.icu. Data received from these services are used only for the coaching features explicitly requested by the user and are handled according to the applicable platform terms.

When an athlete connects Intervals.icu, the coach may create and update planned structured workouts in that athlete’s calendar, and synchronize supported athlete settings such as thresholds and training zones, within the permissions explicitly granted by the athlete.

One activity source can be selected for imports. Workout destinations are selected separately. Disconnecting a service stops future access through that connection but does not automatically delete the history already stored in the coach.

Artificial intelligence and Garmin data

Selected profile information, activity metrics, plans, feedback and coaching conversations are processed through the OpenAI API to generate personalized training plans and responses. AI output can be inaccurate and is not medical advice. Garmin-derived data require an explicit AI consent in the Garmin card under Connections. That consent is not preselected and can be withdrawn there for future processing. Without it, new AI processing for a profile containing Garmin data is suspended; local consultation and sending previously saved workouts remain available. Garmin data and derived content are not intended for training, evaluating or improving AI models.

5. Legal basis

Where applicable, data are processed on the basis of the user's consent, the performance of a requested service, and legitimate interests in maintaining the security and reliability of the platform.

6. Data sharing

Personal data are not sold. Data may be processed by technical service providers only when necessary to operate the platform, such as hosting, messaging, artificial intelligence or connected fitness services. The coach stores data on a server managed by the controller. Cloudflare provides HTTPS and tunnel services; OpenAI processes the information needed for AI coaching; Stripe processes payments; connected fitness platforms and Telegram receive information needed for the connections and features you choose.

When device temperature is unavailable, Open-Meteo may receive approximate activity coordinates, date and time to estimate temperature and humidity. We do not send Open-Meteo names, user IDs, heart-rate data or full GPS tracks. Third-party providers may process data outside Switzerland under their applicable service and privacy terms.

7. Data retention

Data are retained only for as long as required to provide the service, maintain legitimate records or comply with legal obligations. Users may delete their coach account and stored data from Athlete Profile in the web app, or request deletion by contacting us. Deleting a coach account does not delete the user’s accounts or data held by connected third-party services.

8. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect personal data, including controlled access, secure authentication and separation of user data.

9. User rights

Users can export a copy of their profile from the web app. They may also request access, correction, export or deletion of their personal data and may withdraw consent for connected services.
